The model catalog lists Anthropic's current models only (Claude Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5 and Opus 5). OpenAI, Google, Mistral and xAI models still run with their own keys, but `doctor` warns that they are untested. Until they are listed, they are best reached through OpenRouter or LiteLLM. `doctor` names the listed IDs when an Anthropic ID isn't one of them, a likely typo. See docs/models.md.
