`document` offers to reuse an earlier analysis of the same, unchanged issue (step `reuse`, as in `test-cases`). `--publish-from <run id>` (inputs `documentRun` and `publish: confluence`) publishes an earlier run's reviewed document to Confluence in a new run, with nothing analyzed or drafted again. The `done` step of `document` says where the document is and which Confluence page was written.
